How it works

Three steps. One book, yours alone.

  1. i

    Tell us the idea

    A sentence, a page of notes, or everything you have, documents included. Choose fiction or nonfiction and a length, from a short story to an epic.

  2. ii

    We write and typeset it

    Your book is written, edited and typeset for you alone, with chapters, a cover and front matter. Name it yourself or pick one of our suggested titles.

  3. iii

    A printed copy and the ebook arrive

    The ebook is in your library within about a day. Your paperback or hardcover is printed and bound like any book on your shelf, then shipped to you.

Delivery

What to expect, and when.

Writing
Finishes within about a day of your order; shorter books are often ready sooner. We email you the moment your book is ready, and the ebook is in your library.
Printing
Your printed copy takes 3–5 business days to print and bind once the book is finished.
Shipping
Then it ships at the speed you chose: Standard (5–8 days), Ground (3–5), Expedited (2–3) or Express (1–2).

Refunds & cancellation

Clear terms, before you pay.

  • Cancel free until writing begins. Writing usually begins within an hour of ordering; until then you can cancel for a full refund.
  • Once writing begins, the order is final. Each book is written for one customer and can’t be resold.
  • If a printed copy can’t be produced, we refund the printing and shipping, and the ebook stays yours.
  • If we can’t take on your book, because it falls among the few subjects we don’t write, we tell you promptly and refund the order in full.

To cancel or ask about an order, write to hello@crispybooks.xyz.

Questions

What people ask first.

Is it really a whole book?

Yes. A finished, structured book with chapters, a cover, front matter and, for nonfiction, a sources section, typeset to the length you chose. It reads and prints like any book on your shelf.

Who writes it?

Crispy Books does. Each book is written from your description by our in-house editorial process, edited for consistency, checked against its sources where it makes factual claims, and typeset by us. Your book is written once, for you, and never resold.

Can I choose the title and put my name on it?

Yes. Set the title and subtitle yourself or pick one of three suggestions written for your book. You can print a name on the cover and title page as “inspired by”.

Can I order more printed copies later?

Yes. Every book stays in your library, and you can order more copies in either binding at any time.
