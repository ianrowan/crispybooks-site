Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026

This policy explains what Crispy Books (“we”, “us”) collects about you when you use our website and order a book, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. We have kept it short and plain on purpose. If anything is unclear, write to hello@crispybooks.xyz.

1. What we collect

  • Your account: your email address, your name if your sign-in method provides it, and a password or a Google or Apple sign-in, handled by Clerk, our sign-in provider. We never see your password.
  • Your order: the idea you describe, any reference text you upload, the cover image you upload or choose, the title and subtitle, the name you ask us to print as “inspired by”, the length, genre and binding you choose, and the shipping name, address and phone number you enter for the carrier.
  • Your payment: your card details go directly to Stripe, our payment provider. We receive confirmation of the payment, the amount, the card brand and its last four digits, and a Stripe reference. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Your book: the text, cover and files we produce for you, and the record of its progress and of your order.
  • Email delivery: whether the emails we send you were delivered, through Resend, our email provider.
  • Technical details: your IP address and browser details in our server logs, used for security and to keep the service running, and, when something goes wrong, a report of the error and the page it happened on, through Sentry, our error-reporting provider.
  • How the site is used: the pages you visit, the step of an order you reach, and the buttons you use, through PostHog, our analytics provider. This is collected without cookies and without a profile of you: we see that someone reached a step, not who. Meta also receives the pages you visit on this site and, when you buy, the order amount, through its Pixel in your browser and its Conversions API from our servers, so that we can measure our advertising. You can turn both off for your browser with the switch on the app’s privacy page, under “Your choices and rights”.
  • Correspondence: what you write to us at hello@crispybooks.xyz.

2. How we use it

  • To write, typeset, print and deliver your book, and to show it in your library.
  • To take payment, release holds, issue refunds, and keep the accounts the law requires.
  • To email you about your order: when it is received, when the book is ready, when a copy ships, and if something needs your attention. We send no marketing email.
  • To answer you when you write to us.
  • To understand how the site is used and how our advertising performs.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent fraud and abuse, and meet our legal obligations.
  • To improve the service, using what we learn from orders as a whole.

Books we have written may be reproduced and sold by us, as the Terms of Service explain. Where a book draws on personal details you gave us, we may remove or change names and details in the copies we distribute, and you can ask us not to distribute a particular book.

3. Who we share it with

We share personal information only with the providers that help us run the service, each under a contract that limits what they may do with it:

  • Clerk, for accounts and sign-in: your name, email address and sign-in details.
  • Stripe, for payments: your card details, which you enter directly with Stripe, your email address and the order amount.
  • Lulu, for printing and shipping: the shipping name, address and phone number, and the book files, for every printed copy. Lulu passes the address to the carrier.
  • Resend, for email: your email address and the content of the emails we send you.
  • Amazon Web Services, for hosting and storage: our servers, database and file storage, in the United States, hold everything above.
  • PostHog, for analytics: the pages you visit and the steps you take on the site, and the IP address and browser details that reach it with every request, without cookies or a profile of you.
  • Meta Platforms, for advertising measurement: the pages you visit, the order amount when you buy, the identifiers Meta’s Pixel keeps in your browser, and, from our servers, a hashed form of your email address, your IP address and your browser details, so that a purchase can be matched to an advertisement. Meta uses this under its own data policy.
  • Sentry, for error reports: the error, the page it happened on and your browser details, never what you typed.
  • The artificial intelligence providers we use under contract to help write and typeset books, which process the idea and material you send us for that purpose only.

We also disclose information when the law requires it, to protect our rights or safety or anyone else’s, and as part of a sale or merger of the business, in which case this policy continues to apply. We do not sell personal information. Sending Meta the measurement information above may count as “sharing” under California law; you can opt out under “Your choices and rights”, and we honor the Global Privacy Control signal.

4. Cookies

We use the cookies the service needs to work: the session cookies from Clerk that keep you signed in, and the cookies Stripe sets inside its checkout to process your payment and prevent fraud. Our analytics set no cookies. Meta’s Pixel sets its own cookies for advertising measurement, unless you have turned it off with the switch on the app’s privacy page or your browser sends the Global Privacy Control signal. You can clear cookies in your browser; you will be signed out.

5. Where your information is processed

We are based in the United States and process information there. If you are in the United Kingdom, the European Economic Area or elsewhere, your information is transferred to the United States. We protect it with contracts with our providers that include the standard contractual terms the law provides for such transfers.

6. How long we keep it

  • Your account and your books: for as long as your account is open, so your library and your files stay available.
  • Order and payment records: seven years after the order, as tax and accounting law requires, even if you close your account.
  • Server logs: up to ninety days.
  • Correspondence: up to two years after our last exchange.

When you close your account, we delete your account, your library and your book files within thirty days, except for the records above and copies we are required to keep.

7. Your choices and rights

You can see and download your books in your library at any time, and change your account details through the account menu.

You can ask us to access, correct, delete or export the personal information we hold about you, to restrict or object to how we use it, or to withdraw consent where we rely on it, by writing to hello@crispybooks.xyz. We answer within thirty days and may need to confirm who you are. If you are in the United Kingdom or the European Economic Area, you may also complain to your data protection authority. California residents have the rights the California Consumer Privacy Act provides, including the right to opt out of the sharing described above; the switch on the app’s privacy page and the Global Privacy Control signal are how to exercise it. We do not sell personal information.

Analytics and advertising measurement: the switch on the app’s privacy page turns both off for the browser you use it in. We also honor the Global Privacy Control signal: when your browser sends it, both are off without any action from you.

The switch is on the app’s privacy page; it is not on this site, which runs no analytics or advertising measurement.

Deleting your account does not remove a book from your own devices, and it does not change the rights in the book described in the Terms of Service.

8. Children

The service is for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has given us information, write to hello@crispybooks.xyz and we will delete it.

9. Security

Connections to the site are encrypted. Passwords are handled by Clerk and card details by Stripe, and neither reaches our servers. Access to our systems is limited to the people who run the service. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password and tell us at once if you suspect a problem.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top says when it last changed, and we announce material changes on the site or by email before they take effect.

11. Contact

Crispy Books, New York, NY. Privacy questions and requests: hello@crispybooks.xyz.
